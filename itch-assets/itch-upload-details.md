## 4. Description (HTML Code for Itch.io Editor)
*Copy and paste the HTML block below directly into the Itch.io page description field (HTML mode).*

```html
<h1>🎭 The Fence-Sitter🎭</h1>
<h4>Performative Apathy Generator&nbsp;</h4>
<blockquote>Say everything. Mean nothing. Master the art of PR-approved apathy.</blockquote>

<h2>📝 Introduction</h2>
<p>In a world overflowing with global crises, systemic issues, and urgent calls for accountability, our beloved celebrities and people in power have made a brave collective choice: <strong>to completely disassociate from reality</strong>.</p>
<p>Why take a genuine stance when you have a brand to protect, sponsorships to maintain, and follower counts to preserve? However, crafting a statement that <em>looks</em> deeply concerned while taking no actual position takes precious time away from your luxury retreat.</p>
<p>To reduce the burden on our hardworking elites, <strong>The Fence-Sitter</strong> was built to help. If you are a celebrity, influencer, corporate executive, or anyone who wants to be masterfully performative while remaining truly apathetic, use this tool for all your public relations ideas.</p>

<h2>✨ What It Is & How It Works</h2>
<p><strong>The Fence-Sitter</strong> is a web-based satirical generator designed to craft ultra-diplomatic, non-committal PR statements instantly.</p>
<ul>
  <li><strong>Choose Your Controversy:</strong> Select from trending global topics, social dilemmas, or corporate scandals.</li>
  <li><strong>Set Your Performative Tone:</strong> Fine-tune your stance&mdash;ranging from "Vague Thoughts & Prayers" to "Aggressively Neutral" and "Both Sides Have Valid Points".</li>
  <li><strong>Pick Your Aesthetic:</strong> Choose iconic celebrity post styles, including the classic iOS Notes App screenshot, dark mode story aesthetic, or official PR text.</li>
  <li><strong>Export & Post:</strong> Download your statement as a social-media-ready image or copy the text directly. Zero genuine effort required!</li>
</ul>
```
